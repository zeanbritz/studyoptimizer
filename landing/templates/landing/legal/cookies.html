What cookies do

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. StudiGarden, operated by {{ legal_name }} (registration number {{ registration_number }}), uses essential cookies so accounts, forms and security protections work correctly.

Cookies set by StudiGarden

  • sessionid: remembers your signed-in session and server-side session information. It normally lasts for up to two weeks, but can end earlier when you sign out or the session is invalidated.
  • csrftoken: helps prevent another website from submitting a form as you. It normally lasts for up to one year and may be renewed when you use the site.

Cookie duration can be shortened by browser settings, security action, account deletion or changes needed to protect the service.

Optional cookies

Your browser controls
