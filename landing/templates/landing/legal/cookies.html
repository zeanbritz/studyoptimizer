What cookies do

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. The current Django application uses session and cross-site request forgery (CSRF) protection mechanisms to help users stay signed in and protect forms.

Essential cookies

  • Session cookie: helps the website remember your signed-in session.
  • CSRF cookie: helps protect form submissions from unauthorized requests.
